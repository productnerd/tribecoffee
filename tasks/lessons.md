# Lessons

- **Never use Caveat or Caveat Brush.** Maria finds them horrible (2026-10-15). Offer better handwriting fonts and let her compare them on the page.
- **Illustrations come from Higgsfield, not code.** A texture I drew as SVG line art was rejected as "programmatically drawn" (2026-10-15). Generate real illustrations, then post-process (tone, tiling) in code if needed.
- **Check title edges after changing how they paint.** Spektra's caps (and É) rise above its tight line-heights; a `background-clip: text` gradient cropped every title's top until padded (2026-10-15).
- **"More opacity" on the background means fainter.** Maria uses it to mean more subtle (2026-10-15).
