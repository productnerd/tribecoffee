Our
story

from green bean to golden roast

It starts on a farm, far from here.

Our journey begins with green coffee, sourced from some of the world's finest farmers in Brazil, Ethiopia, Costa Rica and beyond.

We buy from rural farming communities in some of the poorest economies in the world. That way every bag gives something back to the people who grew it, and great coffee still reaches your cup.

Then we roast it ourselves at Agias Zonis in Limassol, for the cafés, restaurants, offices and kitchens of Cyprus.
